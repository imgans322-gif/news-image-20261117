A bridge falls in Crimea hours before three presidents sit down in China; drones cross the Gulf, Gaza braces for winter, measles returns to America, and climate negotiators race the clock in Antalya. This is Tuesday, 17 November 2026, summed up in a single picture.

What the picture shows

Each panel is one of the day's most consequential stories, as reported on 17 November 2026.

EUROPE · WAR

Ukraine topples the Kerch Bridge arch

A Ukrainian missile-and-drone strike — the second in two days, inside a record 1,441-drone barrage — dropped the road arch of the bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea into the Kerch Strait. Two people were killed, road traffic was suspended, and Moscow promised repairs.

ASIA · DIPLOMACY

Trump, Xi & Putin to meet in China

The US, Chinese and Russian presidents are set to hold their first trilateral meeting on Thursday in Shenzhen, on the sidelines of the APEC summit — with the Ukraine war at the top of the agenda, two days after the Kerch Bridge fell.

MIDDLE EAST

Iranian drones aimed at U.S. base in Bahrain

Bahrain said its air defences intercepted Iranian drones targeting the American military radar site at Sheikh Isa air base, home to the US Fifth Fleet. Washington condemned the attack as the Strait of Hormuz crisis deepened.

HUMANITARIAN

Gaza faces a winter emergency

UN agencies warned that more than 1.7 million people remain displaced in Gaza and about 1.4 million face acute hunger as winter closes in, while Britain, Australia, Canada and Italy urged Israel to open the crossings to aid.

AMERICAS · HEALTH

U.S. loses measles elimination status

The United States lost its measles-free status after 26 years, with more than 5,400 cases and 10 deaths reported this year — the worst numbers since the early 1990s, amid falling vaccination rates.

CLIMATE · COP31

COP31 talks enter decisive phase

The COP31 climate conference in Antalya, Türkiye, entered its decisive stage, with negotiators working on the first draft of a possible agreement and a new AI-for-climate pledge launched with the ITU.
